Avatar identifies a person — a customer or the operator — by photo or initials.

**You provide** `name` (always; it is the accessible label and the source of initials) and optionally `src`. Initials are first and last name.

Sizes: `sm` 24 in dense lists and message threads, `md` 32 in table rows and the sidebar footer, `lg` 44 in list items and conversation headers, `xl` 64 in the contact inspector header.

**Don't** colour avatars per person; the fill is always `surface-sunken` so photos and initials sit together quietly.

Tokens: `radius-pill`, `surface-sunken` fill, `ink` initials at 600.
